    How fairness works

    1. Every participant gets one vote.
    2. Votes are counted equally.
    3. The initiative with the most votes becomes the majority choice once the minimum participation threshold is met.
    4. Group participation is displayed so smaller groups stay visible.
    5. Underrepresentation warnings never secretly alter votes.
    6. A tie is shown openly, not hidden.
    7. The system does not claim the majority speaks for everyone when participation is limited.

    How the majority rule works

    1. Participants select one initiative.
    2. JavaScript counts each vote.
    3. Percentages are calculated from total votes.
    4. The initiative with the most votes becomes the current majority choice.
    5. If two or more initiatives share the highest count, a tie is displayed.
    6. A minimum participation threshold can be required before a final decision.
    7. Group-level participation is displayed to make representation visible.
