04 Transparency

How fairness works

Majority rule provides a transparent way to aggregate preferences, while participation data and inclusion safeguards help reveal whether some groups are underrepresented.

01

One person, one vote

Every participant gets exactly one vote, counted equally regardless of group.

02

Group participation visible

Participation from each community group is displayed, so smaller groups remain visible.

03

Underrepresentation monitored

Warnings appear when a group has low participation — but never change the vote count.

04

Participation threshold

A minimum of 10 participants must be met before declaring a final community decision.

05

Ties shown transparently

If initiatives tie, the tie is displayed openly. No winner is ever invented.

06

No silent adjustments

The safeguard provides visibility — it does not alter individual votes.

05 Global Goals

SDG impact & documentation

EQUALECT connects directly to two United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

5 Gender Equality

SDG 5 — Gender Equality

EQUALECT supports women's participation and visibility in collective decision-making. The Women's Digital Skills Program and Safe Transport Campaign address digital access and physical safety. Group-level participation tracking ensures women's voices are counted and seen.

10 Reduced Inequalities

SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities

By making participation differences visible and providing inclusion safeguards, the project works against smaller or underrepresented groups disappearing from the overall result. The Accessible Learning Center and Inclusive Job Fair directly reduce barriers for people with disabilities and low-income workers.

How the majority rule works

  1. 1Participants select one initiative.
  2. 2JavaScript counts each vote equally.
  3. 3Percentages are calculated from total votes.
  4. 4The initiative with the most votes becomes the current majority choice.
  5. 5If two or more initiatives tie, the system displays a tie — no winner is invented.
  6. 6A minimum participation threshold (10 participants) must be met before declaring a final decision.
  7. 7Group-level participation is displayed to make representation visible.
