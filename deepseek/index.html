Transparency

How Fairness Works

Majority rule provides a transparent way to aggregate preferences, while participation data and inclusion safeguards help reveal whether some groups are underrepresented.

✓

One person, one vote

Every participant gets exactly one vote. Votes are counted equally, regardless of group.

👁️

Group participation visible

Participation from each community group is displayed, so smaller groups remain visible.

⚠️

Underrepresentation monitored

Warnings appear when a group has very low participation — but never secretly change the vote.

📋

Minimum participation threshold

A configurable threshold (10 participants) must be met before declaring a final decision.

⚖️

Ties shown transparently

If two initiatives have equal votes, a tie is displayed openly. No winner is invented.

Global Goals

SDG Impact

EQUALECT connects directly to two United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

5
Gender Equality

This application supports the participation and visibility of women's preferences in collective decision-making. The Women's Digital Skills Program and group-level participation tracking ensure women's voices are counted and seen.

10
Reduced Inequalities

By making participation differences visible and providing inclusion safeguards, the project works against smaller or underrepresented groups disappearing from the overall result. Transparency replaces silent exclusion.

🗳️ How the Majority Rule Works

  1. Participants select one initiative.
  2. JavaScript counts each vote.
  3. Percentages are calculated from total votes.
  4. The initiative with the most votes becomes the current majority choice.
  5. If two or more initiatives have the same highest number of votes, the system displays a tie.
  6. A minimum participation threshold (10 participants) must be met before declaring a final decision.
  7. Group-level participation is displayed to make representation visible.
